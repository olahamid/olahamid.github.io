Modulo Labs · gatta.io

smart contract engineer / security researcher

Built an Intel TDX attestation and zero-knowledge stack that ties each campaign's reward root to the code that produced it, verified on-chain. Reviewed every release.

Technical detail
  • TDX quotes via the Phala dstack SDK (getQuote). Each quote's reportData commits to keccak256(merkleRoot, totalPoints, campaignHash), and the app's RTMR3 is registered on-chain at campaign creation.
  • A Rust proof worker fetches collateral from Automata's on-chain PCCS and runs Automata's DCAP verifier inside the SP1 zkVM, turning a ~5 KB quote into a ~256-byte Groth16 proof verified on-chain. PostgreSQL-backed job pipeline with row-level locking, idempotent submission and retries.
  • A Noir (UltraHonk) circuit for anonymous claims, with Solidity verifiers on Sepolia, Base Sepolia and Horizen, proving depth-16 Poseidon Merkle membership, bound to a payout address by a secp256k1 ECDSA signature.
  • Four audit reports on the stack: 17 findings (3 High, 1 Medium, 5 Low, 8 Info/Gas), plus a review of removing SP1 and an end-to-end analysis of the TEE, proof worker and contracts.

Closed source, but I'm happy to walk through it on a call.
